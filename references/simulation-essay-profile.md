# simulation-essay-v1 profile

Use this profile for a connected visual essay in which one model accumulates
meaning across scenes. Benchmark the progressive construction, direct
manipulation, visual continuity, and prose-to-figure alignment of strong
simulation essays such as <https://ciechanow.ski/gps/> without copying their
code, assets, branding, or exact composition.

## Storyboard before implementation

For every scene record privately:

1. Question created by the previous scene.
2. Learner action or state link.
3. Immediate visible response.
4. Invariant that remains fixed.
5. Insight made observable.
6. State and visual vocabulary carried forward.

Reject a scene when its visual can be removed without weakening the causal
explanation. Do not convert an editorial outline into a stack of large charts.

## Model continuity

- Reuse one model state across connected scenes when the concept permits it.
- Introduce one new state variable or representation at a time.
- Reuse semantic colors, object identities, labels, camera conventions, and
  units across the page.
- Prefer direct manipulation of the represented object. Mirror it with a native
  or keyboard-operable control.
- Use in-prose state links when a sentence names a useful representative case.
- Synchronize multiple views when their relationship is the point.
- Keep important evidence visible long enough to compare before and after.

## Layout and motion

Keep prose in a readable editorial column. Let a simulation expand beyond that
column only when the spatial or comparative model benefits. On mobile, recompose
labels, controls, camera, and view hierarchy; do not uniformly shrink a dense
desktop scene.

Animation must show causality, interpolation, or time evolution. Provide pause,
replay, reset, and reduced-motion behavior when motion carries meaning. Stop
expensive animation when its scene is offscreen.

## Acceptance gate

- The initial pose is informative without interaction.
- At least three connected scenes reuse the declared model or visual vocabulary.
- The learner can point to the evidence for every abstract claim.
- Model calculations and reference states pass an independent oracle.
- Representative, intermediate, and extreme states remain internally consistent.
- Direct manipulation, native controls, keyboard input, and live text agree.
- No-JavaScript text explains the central relationship.
- The page states the model's assumptions and limits near the model.
