# editorial-v1 profile

Use this contract only when `editorial-v1` is the selected production profile.
It draws on the calm editorial rhythm of <https://declaude.org/watermarking/>
without copying source code, branding, prose, diagrams, or distinctive assets.

## Required page rhythm

1. Use one centered reading column, approximately 722px or 45.125rem wide.
2. Start with a modest title and two or three short framing paragraphs. Do not
   add a full-screen marketing hero or visible metadata for inferred difficulty,
   audience, reading time, or generic educational simplification.
3. Organize the body into four to seven numbered steps. Each step contains:
   - a compact numbered heading;
   - one muted goal callout with a left accent rule;
   - a short explanation;
   - an interaction or figure when manipulation adds understanding;
   - an immediate plain-language takeaway.
4. Keep interactions inside the prose flow. Avoid side navigation, dashboard
   chrome, unrelated card grids, KPI tiles, and persistent toolbars.

## Required visual tokens

- Page: warm off-white `#faf9f6`.
- Main text: near-black `#1a1a18`.
- Muted text: `#6b6862`.
- Accent: burnt orange `#b3541e`.
- Rules: warm gray `#e3e0d8`.
- Figure surface: white with a 1px rule and 12px radius.
- Inset surface: `#faf8f1`, optionally with a thin accent rule.
- Typeface: native system sans-serif for prose.
- Base type: about 17px with roughly 1.65 line height on desktop.
- Content width: 45.125rem maximum with comfortable mobile gutters.

Use additional semantic colors only when the subject requires them. Never make
color the sole carrier of meaning.

## Controls and motion

- Primary buttons: near-black fill, off-white text, 8px radius.
- Secondary buttons: transparent fill, warm-gray border, muted text, 8px radius.
- Keep controls compact and directly under the model they affect.
- Show changed state in the figure and explain it in nearby live text.
- Use short causal transitions. Avoid ambient animation, parallax, decorative
  gradients, glass effects, blobs, and heavy shadows.
- Preserve visible focus, reduced motion, keyboard operation, and touch targets.

Adapt marks, diagrams, and mechanics to the content while preserving the page
shell. Accessibility requirements always override visual mimicry.
